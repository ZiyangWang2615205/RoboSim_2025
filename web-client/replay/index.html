Instructions

Left Click & Drag Rotating view

Right Click & Drag Panning view

Mouse Wheel Scroll Zoom in/out

Use the dropdown in the bottom-right corner to choose which traveller box ID to target.

Top-Down View places the camera directly above the selected target.

Following View locks the camera to the selected target and follows it in third-person. Can rotate view by left click & drag.

Transparent Box makes all boxes transparent except the traveller box.

Play pauses or resumes the replay. (Not available for live view.)
